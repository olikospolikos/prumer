Accessibility at Boldo

Last reviewed: 26 August 2026

Boldo is about walking up to a stranger and saying something. A website that locks people out is a poor advertisement for that. This page says plainly what we have built, what we have actually tested, and what is still broken.

Partially conformant

Conformance target

We aim to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. "Partially conformant" means most of the standard is met, but not all of it. The gaps we know about are listed below rather than papered over.

This statement covers the Boldo waitlist site at estudivo.cz/boldo. The Boldo mobile app ships separately; its accessibility work is summarised at the bottom of this page.

What this site does

  • Text contrast. Every text and background pair on this site meets or exceeds the 4.5:1 AA ratio for body text. The lowest pair currently measures 4.85:1.
  • Keyboard. Everything interactive can be reached and operated with a keyboard alone, in a sensible order, with a visible focus ring on each stop. A skip link jumps past the header.
  • Labels. Form fields have real, visible <label> elements. Not placeholder text, which vanishes the moment you start typing.
  • Errors. Validation messages are announced by screen readers, are tied to the field they describe, and never rely on colour alone to signal a problem.
  • Success. After you join the waitlist, focus moves to the confirmation heading, so screen reader users hear that it worked.
  • Motion. If your system asks for reduced motion, the entrance animations and smooth scrolling switch off.
  • Zoom and reflow. The page reflows to a single column and stays usable at 200% zoom and at 320px wide.
  • Structure. Headings run in order, landmarks are marked up, and the example challenge inside the phone mock is readable by a screen reader instead of being hidden from it.

Being straight with you: an accessibility statement is not the same thing as an accessible site, and automated checks are not the same thing as testing. The last two rows above are the ones that find the barriers a checklist misses, and we have not done them yet. Until we have, treat the list above as our best effort rather than a guarantee.

Known limitations

  • No screen reader test pass. The markup is written to be announced correctly, but nobody has yet listened to the whole page through VoiceOver, NVDA or TalkBack. There may be barriers here that only show up in real use.
  • Decorative background. The warm glow behind the page is hidden from assistive technology on purpose. It carries no information.
  • The phone mock is an illustration. Its example challenge is announced as text, but it is a picture of the product, not live content. The surrounding chrome - status bar, progress bar, tab bar - is hidden.
  • Confirmation email. The waitlist confirmation email is sent through a third party. We have not audited its HTML for screen reader friendliness.
  • English only. This site is not currently translated, though the app itself ships in English, Czech and Spanish.

The Boldo app

The mobile app is built against the same WCAG 2.1 AA target, adapted to iOS and Android conventions:

  • All controls carry accessibility roles, labels and selection state, so VoiceOver and TalkBack announce what they are and whether they are chosen.
  • Text colours meet the same 4.5:1 minimum as this site.
  • The app honours the system "Reduce Motion" setting throughout its animations and its onboarding.
  • Charts and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers, with the same information given in text next to them.
  • Known gap: the app has not yet had a full screen reader test pass either, and dynamic type support above the largest system size is untested.

Found a barrier? Tell us

This is the part that actually matters. If something on this site or in the app blocks you, we want to hear it, including the boring details, like which screen reader and browser you were using and what you expected to happen.

Email olikoskral@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line. We aim to reply within 5 working days and to tell you either when it will be fixed or why it will not be.

If you would rather not email, say so in your message and we will find another way to reach you.

Enforcement

Boldo is operated from the Czech Republic. If you are in the EU and you are not satisfied with how we have handled your report, you can escalate to your national accessibility enforcement body. In the Czech Republic that is the Ministry of the Interior (mvcr.cz).
